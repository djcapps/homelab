# Media notes

The photographs, screenshots, and recordings come from my lab. Public copies are separate from the originals.

## Photographs

`desk-area.jpg` has restrained AI-assisted retouching: lighting correction, removal of loose background cable loops, and removal of two framed personal photographs. Screen widgets, small displays, and address labels are covered for privacy. It is a view of the workspace, not evidence of its cable routing or live dashboard readings.

`mini-rack.jpg` uses the original rack photograph, cropped with opaque redactions over private screen headings, address stickers, and disk identifiers. Its equipment and wiring have not been retouched.

`gpu-workstations.jpg` uses the original photograph of the two towers and UPS, cropped without object removal or generative editing.

The source photographs remain unchanged. Published copies were compressed and stripped of source EXIF, GPS, embedded thumbnails, and other metadata.

## Screenshots and recordings

Screenshots accompany [Homepage](../projects/homepage.md), [Orrery](../projects/network-orrery.md), [desktop tools](../projects/desktop-tools.md), [mobile access](../projects/mobile-access.md), [monitoring](../projects/monitoring.md), [Proxmox](../projects/proxmox.md), [GPU inference](../projects/gpu-inference.md), and [Kuma](../kubernetes/uptime-kuma/README.md). Cropping removes unused space and unrelated UI. The readings, error messages, and service states have not been rewritten.

The Orrery media keeps ordinary host names, service labels, private LAN addresses, and metrics visible. Only an unrelated personal name is covered in the rack captures. The six phone captures cover that same name and the personal email address in Windows system information. The wake and Claude Code captures need no redaction. Some other dashboard captures also cover addresses and host labels, and camera-event history remains private.

Homepage has an eight-second scrolling view and a nineteen-second SSH launcher recording. The rack Orrery preview is an eight-second excerpt. The widget recording is about seven seconds. The workstation Orrery has six- and seven-second GIF previews at 1,280 pixels wide. Their linked MP4s show about 35 seconds of the black hole at source resolution and thirteen seconds of the planet view at 2,560 pixels wide. The black-hole MP4 adds one pixel of padding at the right and bottom for video encoding. Recording controls at the beginning of the source GIF are omitted.

Clips retain the source playback speed, use reduced frame rates where needed, and contain no audio. The GIF previews link to H.264 MP4 copies, with still frames available for readers who prefer them. These SDR recordings are not HDR demonstrations or performance benchmarks.

Original terminal recordings, browser bookmarks, and long source recordings remain private. The public SSH animation covers login details, host labels, and addresses. The repository contains compressed derivatives rather than hundreds of megabytes of source media.

The Orrery images are captures of running visualization software, not depictions of physical equipment. The workstation renderer includes procedural and modeled artwork, including a decorative dragon. Its project credits include NASA/GSFC star-map imagery, Tycho-2, and HYG catalog data. Third-party applications, fonts, and artwork retain their respective rights. The repository's code license does not relicense photographs, screenshots, or recordings.
