# Photographs

All three photographs were taken in my lab on November 10, 2026.

`desk-area.jpg` has restrained AI-assisted retouching: lighting correction, removal of loose background cable loops, and removal of two framed personal photographs. Screen widgets, small displays, and address labels are covered for privacy. It is a view of the workspace, not evidence of its cable routing or live dashboard readings.

`mini-rack.jpg` uses the original rack photograph, cropped with opaque redactions over private screen headings, address stickers, and disk identifiers. Its equipment and wiring have not been retouched.

`gpu-workstations.jpg` uses the original photograph of the two towers and UPS, cropped without object removal or generative editing.

The source photographs remain unchanged. Published copies were compressed and stripped of source EXIF, GPS, embedded thumbnails, and other metadata. Photographs are not covered by the code license.
