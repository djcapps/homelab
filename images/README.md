# Media notes

The photographs, screenshots, and recordings come from my lab. Public copies are separate from the originals.

## Photographs

`desk-area.jpg` has restrained AI-assisted retouching: lighting correction, removal of loose background cable loops, and removal of two framed personal photographs. Screen widgets, small displays, and address labels are covered for privacy. It is a view of the workspace, not evidence of its cable routing or live dashboard readings.

`mini-rack.jpg` uses the original rack photograph, cropped with opaque redactions over private screen headings, address stickers, and disk identifiers. Its equipment and wiring have not been retouched.

`gpu-workstations.jpg` uses the original photograph of the two towers and UPS, cropped without object removal or generative editing.

The source photographs remain unchanged. Published copies were compressed and stripped of source EXIF, GPS, embedded thumbnails, and other metadata.

## Screenshots and recordings

Screenshots accompany [Homepage](../projects/homepage.md), [Orrery](../projects/network-orrery.md), [desktop tools](../projects/desktop-tools.md), [monitoring](../projects/monitoring.md), [Proxmox](../projects/proxmox.md), [GPU inference](../projects/gpu-inference.md), and [Kuma](../kubernetes/uptime-kuma/README.md). Cropping removes unused space and unrelated UI. Opaque redactions cover private addresses, host labels, personal names, and camera-event history. The remaining readings, error messages, and service states have not been rewritten.

Homepage has an eight-second scrolling view and a nineteen-second SSH launcher recording. The rack Orrery preview is an eight-second excerpt. The widget recording is about seven seconds. The workstation Orrery clips show an eight-second black-hole view and a six-second planet view; its GIF is a five-second excerpt. Clips retain the source playback speed, use reduced resolution and frame rate, and contain no audio. The GIF previews link to H.264 MP4 copies, with still frames available for readers who prefer them. These SDR recordings are not HDR demonstrations or performance benchmarks.

Original terminal recordings, browser bookmarks, and long source recordings remain private. The public SSH animation covers login details, host labels, and addresses. The repository contains compressed derivatives rather than hundreds of megabytes of source media.

The Orrery images are captures of running visualization software, not depictions of physical equipment. The workstation renderer includes procedural and modeled artwork, including a decorative dragon. Its project credits include NASA/GSFC star-map imagery, Tycho-2, and HYG catalog data. Third-party applications, fonts, and artwork retain their respective rights. The repository's code license does not relicense photographs, screenshots, or recordings.
